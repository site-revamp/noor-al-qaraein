# Noor Al Qaraein – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Noor Al Qaraein is a home appliance repair and maintenance business operating since 2015. Based in Sharjah, the company provides appliance repair, installation, and air-conditioning services across Dubai, Sharjah, and Ajman.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the company's services clearly, helping homeowners identify the appropriate appliance repair solution, and making telephone and WhatsApp enquiries accessible.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Service Organisation
- Presents appliance services in clearly identifiable categories.
- Highlights washing machine, refrigerator, dishwasher, gas cooker, and air-conditioning services.
- Makes it easier for visitors to identify the relevant repair or installation service.

### 2. Improved Contact Accessibility
- Highlights the published telephone and WhatsApp contact number: +971 54 326 4565.
- Prioritises direct contact for customers seeking appliance repair and maintenance.
- Supports a more straightforward enquiry journey.

### 3. Stronger Business Information Presentation
- Highlights the company's establishment in 2015.
- Presents its verified service coverage across Dubai, Sharjah, and Ajman.
- Provides clearer business location and company information to help visitors understand where the company operates.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Content Hierarchy:** Organises business information and appliance services into clearly identifiable sections.
- **Service Presentation:** Gives individual appliance categories and their related services clearer visibility.
- **User Experience:** Focuses on straightforward navigation and accessible telephone and WhatsApp contact options.
- **Local Relevance:** Highlights the company's service areas and Sharjah business location.
- **Overall Appearance:** Establishes a more structured, professional presentation focused on appliance repair, installation, and maintenance.

The redesigned homepage provides Noor Al Qaraein with a clearer business presentation, making its services, operating areas, and contact information easier for potential customers to understand and access.
